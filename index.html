What we check

Geometry against the plans

Heels, overhangs, and ceiling and attic geometry — verified from the room dimensions on the plans, not just the spec table.

Loads

Design loads compared with the plan criteria, including a snow-load check for the project location and any special loads the plans call for.

Hangers and connections

Connector capacity for the load and lumber, and whether the connector physically fits where it has to go.

Girder loads on the layout

Bearing reactions written on the layout where the builder and the support designer need to see them.

Every sheet and schedule

The full plan set is swept sheet by sheet — special trusses, holdowns, and drift loads included, not just the framing plan.

Floors

Floor depth, spacing, and deflection checked against the plans and your plant's criteria.

All we need from you

Just the documents — nothing else:

  • The plan set (architectural + structural, PDF)
  • Your truss layout (PDF)
  • The truss profiles (PDF)
  • Your job code and the project ZIP or county — for the snow-load check — plus whether the package is a QUOTE or an ORDER

No account, no installation, no access to your systems. You send documents; you receive a review.

What you never have to send

  • Your customer's name — use your internal job code instead. That is all we need to track the job.
  • The exact street address — a ZIP code or county is enough for the snow-load check.
  • Prices or costs — if you share your connector inventory, we only need model and allowable loads. Leave the price columns out.
  • Your full standards manual — only the pages with the criteria that apply to truss review.

Files are used only to run your review. They are never shared and never used to train AI models, and they are deleted automatically 30 days after delivery. Full details: How we handle your files.
