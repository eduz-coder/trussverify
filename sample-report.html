Review summary — Maple Street Residence

Job code: MS-101  ·  Package: Roof truss layout and profiles  ·  Stage: QUOTE

Recommendation: Do not release yet — two high-priority items below affect the design and the price.

Sheets reviewed: A1–A4, S1.0–S2.1 (fictional demo set). Configuration: example plant configuration — no real plant standards or inventory.

1 · High

Overhang does not match the plan

The structural eave detail dimensions the truss tail to 1'-3" from the wall reference line. Representative profiles show a 1'-5" tail — a difference of 2" on every affected truss.

Action: Confirm the plan reference, correct the affected trusses, and rerun the profiles.

2 · High

Plan-required special truss is shown as a common truss

Sheet S2.0 and its referenced detail call for a spanning gable truss at the labeled shear wall, including specified sheathing and holdown requirements. The layout label at that location corresponds to a common truss profile.

Action: Change the truss type and apply the schedule and detail requirements before release.

3 · Medium

Girder bearing callout is missing

A girder profile reports a maximum gravity reaction of 9,840 lb at its left bearing. No matching reaction callout was found at that bearing on the layout.

Action: Add the rounded-up reaction at the correct bearing and verify the support below.

4 · Confirm

Connector demand exceeds the configured example inventory

A supported truss reaction exceeds the verified capacity entered for the stocked example connector under the applicable load-duration case. The physical fit at that location also requires confirmation.

Action: Select a manufacturer-approved connector using the plant's current catalog data, or place a special order. Do not release with the example connector.

5 · Medium

Floor deflection is outside the configured limit

One fictional floor profile reports live-load deflection beyond the limit set in the example plant configuration, although the design software reported the truss as passing under its own default settings.

Action: Increase depth, reduce spacing, revise chord specification, or use another design option approved by the designer, then rerun.

Items verified — matches the plans

  • Building overall dimensions match the architectural plan.
  • Main roof pitch matches the elevations.
  • Layout labels match the supplied profile names.
  • No duplicate profile was found in the fictional package.
